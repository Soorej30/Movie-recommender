Neural Networks

Overview

A neural network learns layered, non-linear combinations of movie attributes to predict an outcome, extending the same prediction tasks used in Naive Bayes, decision trees, SVMs, and regression with a more flexible model. It will be compared against those earlier methods on the same labeled and continuous targets to see whether its added flexibility meaningfully improves prediction of a movie's reception or commercial performance, or whether simpler methods perform just as well on this dataset.
